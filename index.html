About these calculations & your privacy

Housing-cost burden uses housing costs divided by gross income before taxes, while the monthly budget uses take-home income. The 30% and 50% thresholds are descriptive benchmarks, not proof that someone can or cannot afford housing. The monthly balance is shown only if take-home income is supplied; missing amounts are not silently treated as zero. Savings coverage equals current emergency savings divided by your entered monthly expenses. These are budgeting estimates, not an eviction prediction, credit score, or financial advice. Daily spending records are displayed separately from the monthly plan; they do not automatically change a calculated monthly budget. Recurring bills are reminders, not automatic transactions. Assistance links are referral sources and may not represent currently open programs.

Financial entries stay in page memory only while this tab is open. They are not stored on the device or uploaded to a server. Refreshing or leaving the page removes them. Download or print a report before leaving. Printing may include sensitive financial details.
